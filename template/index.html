About IISEIC 2026

Where innovation, education, and entrepreneurship meet.

IISEIC 2026 is an international innovation competition open to students, educators, professionals, and global participants to showcase transformative ideas that shape the future of learning, technology, business, and sustainability.

🌐

Global Participation

Join a diverse network of participants and collaborators from local and international communities.

Education Impact

Technology & Innovation

Competition Categories

Four high-impact pathways for transformative ideas.

Educator & Learning Transformation Innovation

Solutions that improve teaching, learning experiences, assessment, and academic engagement.

Business & Digital Economy Innovation

Entrepreneurial concepts, digital platforms, and business models for modern economic ecosystems.

AI, Emerging Technology & Digital Media Innovation

Forward-thinking developments powered by AI, smart systems, immersive media, and advanced tools.

Sustainability & Environmental Innovation

Projects that address climate action, green practices, circular economy, and environmental resilience.

Submission Format

Prepare your entries with clear and professional deliverables.

Abstract Submission

Submit in Word format with key components such as title, problem statement, objective, methodology, innovation value, and expected impact.

Poster Submission

Prepare an A2-sized poster in PDF, JPG, or PNG format with concise visuals, innovation highlights, and supporting data.

Video Submission

Create a compelling 3–5 minute video presentation showcasing the problem, solution, prototype, and market or societal relevance.
