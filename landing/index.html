About IISEIC 2026

IISEIC 2026 is an international innovation competition open to students, educators, professionals, and global participants to showcase transformative ideas that shape the future of learning, technology, business, and sustainability.

Global Participation

Join a diverse network of participants and collaborators from local and international communities.

Education Impact

Celebrate innovations that redefine pedagogy, learner engagement, and educational transformation.

Technology & Innovation

Highlight cutting-edge ideas in AI, digital media, sustainability, and entrepreneurial ecosystems.

Competition Categories

Educator & Learning Transformation Innovation

Solutions that improve teaching, learning experiences, assessment, and academic engagement.

Business & Digital Economy Innovation

Entrepreneurial concepts, digital platforms, and business models for modern economic ecosystems.

AI, Emerging Technology & Digital Media Innovation

Forward-thinking developments powered by AI, smart systems, immersive media, and advanced tools.

Sustainability & Environmental Innovation

Projects that address climate action, green practices, circular economy, and environmental resilience.

Rules and Regulations

General Rules

Eligibility

Open to all from public/private institutions, schools, professionals, and international participants.

Participation Format

  1. Individual or group (maximum 5 members including supervisor).
  2. One project per category.
  3. Organizers may reassign project category if necessary.

Language Requirement

All submissions (abstract, poster, and video) must be in English only.

Physical presentation can be Malay and English.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION (PHYSICAL)

  • Team size (1 to 4 students + 1 project mentor/teacher)
  • No limit on the number of teams from each school
  • Each team is required to showcase their project throughout the event
  • Booth setup will take place on 12 May 2026, from 4:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.
  • Each team is responsible for bringing their own project materials and equipment
  • Judging will be conducted on 13 May 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
  • Evaluation by judges will be carried out through interviews and project presentations at the exhibition booth

Abstract & Publication

Abstract must be submitted in Microsoft Word format (.doc or .docx only).

It should include the following key elements:

  1. Title
  2. Problem Statement
  3. Objective
  4. Target User
  5. Features/Impact
  6. Conclusion

Submitted abstracts will be included in the event’s e-proceedings publication.

Poster Requirement (Physical/Online)

Poster must be in A2 size and submitted in PDF, PPTX, JPG, JPEG, or PNG format.

The poster must clearly include the following content elements:

  1. Background
  2. Problem Statement
  3. Objectives
  4. Methodologies
  5. Impacts
  6. Project Visualisation / Potential Application/ Commercialisation/ Photo
  7. Achievement/ Recognition/ Award

The same approved design must be used for physical printing during the event.

Participants are responsible for printing and bringing their poster unless otherwise stated by the organiser.

Video Requirement (Online)

Each submission must include a presentation video between 3 to 5 minutes in length.

The content of the video must clearly present the following:

  1. Background
  2. Problem Statement
  3. Objective
  4. Methodology
  5. Innovation Impact / Benefit
  6. Conclusion
